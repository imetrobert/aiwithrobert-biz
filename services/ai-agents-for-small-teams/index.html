AI agents for small teams

An AI agent is an assistant set up inside your AI plan to carry out a defined job, such as research or first-line customer replies. It is only offered where your plan supports it, and not every business needs one.

What an agent can do

  • Handle a defined job, such as research or first-line customer replies
  • Run multi-step workflows using the connections your plan includes
  • Pause for human approval wherever a decision matters

When it makes sense

Agents earn their place when a task is frequent, well defined and tedious. I only recommend one when it pays off. For many businesses, a good prompt or a simple workflow is the better answer, and I will say so.

You stay in control

Human approval is built in where it matters, so nothing important happens without you. Limits and access to your data are decided up front.

Scope and cost, explained first

Scope and cost depend on the tools and connections involved, such as extra services or paid APIs. These are explained before you commit, and there is no public price list.

Where to begin

A free discovery call. We check whether an agent fits your situation or whether something simpler serves you better.
