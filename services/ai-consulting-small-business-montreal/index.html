AI consulting for small businesses in Montréal

AI with Robert for Business helps small businesses and professional practices of about 5 to 20 people find where AI can genuinely save time, and put it to work with the subscriptions they already have.

What the work looks like

It starts with an AI Opportunity Assessment. We review the AI subscriptions you already pay for and how they are used, map your processes, and spot the repetitive tasks that cost the most time.

What you get

  • A prioritized list of opportunities, with time savings and return on investment estimated where the numbers allow
  • A practical roadmap you can follow on your own or with my help
  • Written documentation in plain language, with prompts tailored to your tasks

Independent and platform-neutral

I am not tied to any vendor. I recommend what suits your business, which may be ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, Claude or another affordable tool. What is possible depends on your plan, connectors and integrations, so I confirm what your subscriptions can actually do before suggesting anything.

Who it is for

Professional services, trades, retail, health and wellness practices and other small teams in Greater Montréal. Beginners with no AI subscription yet are welcome too.

How we start

With a free discovery call. We look at the tools you use and the tasks that eat your time, and you decide whether to go further. Starting prices are on the home page, and each engagement is scoped up front.
