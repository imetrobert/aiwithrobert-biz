Practical AI staff training

For larger teams that need more than a single working session, practical training gets your employees confident with the AI tools and workflows you already use, and keeps the results going.

What the training covers

  • Getting more from the subscriptions you already have
  • Building reusable prompts and workflows for everyday tasks
  • Spotting where automation fits
  • Using AI responsibly, with privacy in mind, including Quebec's Law 25
  • Learning to maintain and improve your new workflows yourselves

Privacy points are practical guidance for everyday use, not legal advice.

Built around your work

Sessions use your own tasks and tools rather than generic examples, so people leave with something they can use on Monday morning.

Language

Training can be given in English, in French, or in both, to suit your team.

Where it fits

For a small team, the core offer is a scoped working session that produces documentation and configured workflows. Employee training is a separate, optional service for teams that want to go further.
