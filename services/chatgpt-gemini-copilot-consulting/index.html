ChatGPT, Gemini and Copilot consulting

Most small businesses already have access to an AI assistant, or are about to. The harder part is knowing which one fits, and using it well. I help with both, without selling any platform.

Choosing the right tool

ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Microsoft Copilot and Claude overlap a lot, but they differ in how they connect to the software you use every day. A business that lives in Microsoft 365 may be served differently from one that lives in Google Workspace. I look at your real tools and tasks first.

Getting more from what you already pay for

Many teams use a small fraction of their subscription. We look at how it is used today and where it could take on recurring work, using tailored prompts and simple workflows.

Confirming what is possible

What an assistant can do varies by plan, connectors, API access and third-party services. I confirm what your subscriptions can actually do, and I explain up front when something would need an extra service or a paid API.

  • Configured workflows around your recurring tasks, with a walkthrough
