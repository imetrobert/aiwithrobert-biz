AI workflow automation for small businesses

Workflow automation turns what AI can do into processes that run the same way every week. The goal is simple: less time on repetitive tasks, with a human still in charge.

Examples of what can be automated

  • Processing incoming documents, forms and emails
  • Preparing quotes and proposals from your own templates
  • Sorting customer inquiries and automating follow-ups
  • Building recurring reports and consolidating information

These are illustrations, not limits. We start from your processes.

How it is done

I design solutions around your existing subscriptions and what your plan can do, then set up the workflows with human approval wherever it matters.

What you keep

Every workflow comes with documentation and a walkthrough, so you and your team can keep using it, adjust it and rely on it without me.

Honest about fit

Not every task is worth automating. I tell you plainly when a simple prompt or a checklist is enough, and what your plan can and cannot do before you commit.

Start with a discovery call

We look at the tasks that eat your time and decide together whether automation fits. Scope is agreed up front, with no public price list.
