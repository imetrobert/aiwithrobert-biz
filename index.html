Services

Practical, jargon-free help to get more from the AI tools you already pay for — and to use them safely.

SMB AI ROI Audit & Roadmap

Find where your business is losing time and pinpoint the low-cost AI tools with the best return.

  • For: owners who want clarity before spending on tools or training
  • You get: a workflow review, tool recommendations and a prioritized 90-day plan
  • Format: strategic audit, about 1–2 weeks
  • Outcome: less wasted software spend and 2–3 high-impact wins

Team AI Upskilling & Hands-On Workshops

Help your staff use AI safely to finish everyday tasks faster.

  • For: small teams in admin, sales, marketing and operations
  • You get: an interactive half-day workshop, prompt libraries and cheat sheets for your industry
  • Format: in person or virtual
  • Outcome: practical AI habits your team can start using the next day

Practical Workflow Automation

Streamline repetitive tasks with simple, smart AI workflows that run reliably.

  • For: owners and operations managers with drafting, reporting, inquiries or content to automate
  • You get: workflow setup, step-by-step procedures and team walkthroughs
  • Format: implementation project
  • Outcome: hours back each week on manual administrative work

AI Safe-Use Policy & Governance

Protect client data and your reputation with a straightforward AI policy, without red tape.

  • For: owners who need simple rules for how staff use AI
  • You get: a 2-page acceptable-use policy, a Law 25 privacy checklist and staff guidelines
  • Format: done-for-you policy package
  • Outcome: clear rules your team follows, with privacy built in (practical guidance, not legal advice)
