Free AI feasibility check for a small business

Before buying another AI tool or booking any work, it helps to know whether the plan you already pay for can do the job. A feasibility check answers that question for each task you name, quickly and at no cost.

What a feasibility check is

It is a short check, not a sales call. You say which AI tools your team already uses, how large the team is, whether personal information is involved, and up to three jobs you would like AI to do. You get a verdict for each job.

What it looks at

  • The plan you actually have: what an assistant can do depends on the plan, connectors, API access and third-party services
  • The kind of job: writing, working with files, reusing the same instructions, using your own documents, scheduling, research and similar tasks
  • Whether personal or client information is involved, because that changes what is sensible

What it does not do

It gives verdicts only. It does not promise savings, compare vendors or quote a price. If a job needs something your plan does not include, the verdict says so, and you decide whether to go further.

Why check first

Many small teams pay for a capable assistant and use a fraction of it, or buy a second tool for something the first one already does. Checking first avoids both.

After the check

If you would like a second opinion or help putting a job into practice, you can book a free discovery call. Robert Simon is independent and does not sell any of the platforms.
