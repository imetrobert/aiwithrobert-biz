ChatGPT vs Gemini vs Copilot for a small business

There is no single winner. For a team of about 5 to 20 people, the better question is which tool fits the way you already work. Here are the questions that decide it.

1. Where does your team already work?

If your documents, email and calendar live in Microsoft 365, Copilot is built to work alongside them. If you live in Google Workspace, Gemini is the natural neighbour. If your work is mostly in a browser and a mix of tools, a general assistant such as ChatGPT or Claude may fit better. What is available to you depends on your plan.

2. What tasks do you want help with?

Drafting, summarizing, researching, preparing quotes and organizing information are common. A tool that handles your top two or three tasks well is worth more than one with a long feature list.

3. What can your plan actually do?

Features vary by plan, connectors, API access and third-party services. Check what your own subscription includes before assuming a capability, and before buying another.

4. How will you handle privacy?

Decide what staff may and may not enter into any assistant, and check the privacy settings of the plan you use. This is practical housekeeping, not legal advice.

5. Who will keep it going?

A tool only pays off if people use it. Tailored prompts, a short written guide and a walkthrough matter more than the logo on the tool.

A sensible way to decide

Pick two or three real tasks, try them in the tools you already have access to, and compare the results. I help teams do exactly that, and I do not sell any of these platforms.
