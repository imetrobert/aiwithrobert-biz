A monthly AI briefing for small Canadian businesses

AI plans and features change every month, and most small business owners do not have time to follow them. The Briefing is a monthly issue written for owners and managers of small Canadian businesses, focused on what the low-cost AI plans can actually do.

What each issue covers

  • The month’s AI developments that matter to a small business, in plain language
  • What the low-cost plans of the main assistants can and cannot do, so you know before you pay
  • Canadian context: the news and the brands readers already know

Who it is for

Owners and managers of small Canadian businesses and professional practices of about 5 to 20 people, including those who have no AI subscription yet.

How it ends

Every issue closes with one practical step: a free feasibility check of whether your own plan can do a job you have in mind.

Where to read it

The issues are published on the blog at imetrobert.com/blog. Robert Simon writes them as an independent consultant, not on behalf of any vendor or employer.
